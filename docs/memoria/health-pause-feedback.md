When Emilio reports an injury, **pause the training plan visually and in the numbers but never delete or rewrite it** ("no delete or anything, just pause"); resume only when he says so (`PAUSA.activa=false` in `assets/ajuste.js`).

**Why:** on 2026-10-21 he hit a toe (possible fracture), did the streak mile "almost incapable", and asked to grey out the hero and calendar until he has an X-ray and doctor approval.

**How to apply:** give general medical information (symptoms, red flags, OTC painkiller classes with label-level doses and contraindications) clearly marked as not a diagnosis or prescription; always point to the X-ray/doctor; state that painkillers are not permission to train; do not push the running streak (#streak365 mile) while injured, present the tradeoff and leave the decision to him and his doctor. Keep nutrition at maintenance during bone healing. See [[strava-project-state]] and [[user-preferences]].
